# Reachly — approved logo

Approved direction: Round 3, concept 01 (9 October 2026). Lime tile, dark lowercase r and detached reach dot; paired with the retained bold serif wordmark.

## Assets
- `reachly-logo-black.svg`: primary horizontal lockup for light surfaces.
- `reachly-logo-white.svg`: pure white lettering for dark surfaces; the lime tile and dark r remain unchanged.
- `reachly-icon.svg`: square icon for favicon and profile uses.
- `*-square.svg` and `*-square-2048.png`: full lockup on a square transparent canvas.
- Logo PNGs: widths 512, 1024, and 2048px.
- Icon PNGs: 16, 32, 48, 180, 192, 300, 512, 1024, and 2048px.
- `favicon.ico`: 16/32/48px embedded.
- `site.webmanifest`: icon declarations. Copy the named PNGs alongside it and set paths for the website.

## Use
Keep proportions fixed. Keep clear space of at least one reach-dot diameter around the visible logo. Use the icon alone when the full wordmark would become too small. Do not recolor the r white inside the lime tile, stretch the mark, add shadows, or move the dot. SVG artwork has outlined letters and requires no installed fonts. Transparent PNGs retain alpha outside the artwork; the lime tile is intentionally opaque.

The intended symbol story is a lowercase r with a point extending beyond its shoulder: extending a business's reach. This is Reachly's chosen interpretation.

## Website integration
Use SVG for headers; black lettering on light surfaces and white lettering on dark surfaces. Set a sensible width and automatic height. Use `alt="Reachly"` for an image that names the brand, or an empty alt when adjacent text repeats it. Favicon: `favicon.ico`; Apple touch icon: `reachly-icon-180.png`. The logo package contains no website changes.
