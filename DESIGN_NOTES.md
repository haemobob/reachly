# Reachly — a point becomes a presence

## Shared identity, different visitor jobs

Studio is a Decide/Learn surface: explain the offer, show real work and make the next decision comprehensible. Possibilities is an Explore surface: invite curiosity, demonstrate authored interaction and return to a commercial destination. Neither duplicates the other or creates a mandatory funnel.

The approved Reachly logo remains intact. Separate point-and-orbit graphics tie the experiences together. Cream, ink, deep green and lime come from the identity. Fraunces gives editorial presence; Manrope keeps body copy and controls direct. Italics use an actual font style.

## Studio composition

An unequal opening pairs a two-line statement with a real client website on a tilted working plate, a lime ticket and a separated explanation/action row. Unequal portfolio frames lead into a green statement, service ledger, five-stage process, exhibition invitation and closing project action. Each section has its own compositional job rather than repeating a three-card template.

Supporting routes extend the identity: work collection and factual project notes, five substantive service pages, About, local project-brief tool, Privacy and recovery page. Capture limitations and attribution boundaries are explicit. No outcomes or detailed delivery scopes are invented.

## Possibilities / exhibition 01

1. **Make a mark:** original shader line field around a lime point. Pointer proximity and Focus/Flow/Reach controls change perspective. Authored SVG artwork remains available.
2. **Open the frame:** native scrolling opens an aperture onto real work, then a staggered gallery. Reduced motion exposes the work without a required reveal.
3. **Connect the dots:** a lime chapter explains a four-stage sample handoff. Visitors choose a scenario and run it. This is an in-house study, not a product, integration or claimed client outcome.
4. **Go further:** graphics resolve into an invitation and an explicit Studio return.

Chapter links are native fragments. ScrollTrigger waits for loading/fonts so initial refresh cannot restore stale scroll positions over direct chapter entry. Browser Back remains ordinary. No wheel interception, custom scrolling or compulsory loader is used.

## Motion and resilience

Studio motion supports hierarchy; exhibition motion is part of its subject. The toggle stores only a non-personal preference. System reduced motion wins, and paused sample workflows run immediately. The shader is capped at pixel ratio 1.5, stops offscreen/when hidden and is exclusive to Possibilities. Context loss keeps fallback artwork visible after perspective changes. Back-forward-cache entries retain their live renderer/listeners instead of disposing them on persisted pagehide.

## Browser critique and refinement

Actual desktop and touch journeys were inspected through opening, portfolio/frame, services/connection and resolution. Refinements addressed italic delivery, caption contrast, correct screenshot dimensions, chapter alignment and responsive orbit geometry based on its column instead of an overly tall inherited box. Keyboard containment and renderer/cache recovery were fixed from failing browser tests. Mobile recomposes vertically; responsive checks cover phones through ultra-wide screens at intermediate scroll positions.

## Reference transformation and release

Shopify informed the division of responsibilities and shared wayfinding. Actual SkillUI Adidas captures informed editorial hierarchy and rhythm. The Awwwards survey covered 300 examples in 14 categories; temporal/source studies informed field, aperture and layout thinking. No Shopify/Adidas assets or wholesale reference templates are distributed. The shader and compositions are authored here.
