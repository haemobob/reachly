## The idea

Reachly should feel like a business opening into a wider world—not an agency listing its capabilities.

**Wider Possibilities** is a living editorial website: warm, confident typography; art-directed views of real work; a small visual point that opens into a much larger frame. Its signature comes from Reachly's own identity: the detached reach dot, interpreted in the brand guide as extending a business's reach.

The official logo remains intact. A separate dot-derived graphic becomes the website's storytelling device. It changes role across the journey: punctuation → opening → connection → invitation. This is not a floating orb added for decoration, and it is not an interactive toy visitors must solve.

The business message stays simple: Reachly helps Indonesian businesses build a credible digital presence and useful systems through websites, digital presence, strategy, automation, and SEO. The presentation can be exceptional without making the business feel expensive, obscure, or difficult to approach.

**Primary surface:** Decide / Learn. **Secondary surface:** Explore, for the work archive.

## Art direction

### Warm editorial scale, precise digital craft

The visual language sits between an independent design publication and a carefully composed exhibition. Large type carries the identity; project imagery provides substance; restrained annotations make it easy to navigate.

| Element | Creative decision |
|---|---|
| Main canvas | Cream `#FAF7F0`: generous, welcoming, slightly print-like |
| Typography and structural contrast | Ink `#05100E` |
| Signature event color | Lime `#D4E751`: punctuation, active connection, and the closing invitation |
| Reflective chapter | Deep green `#17463A`, with cream or white type |
| Display type | Fraunces: expressive, weighty serif, chosen to converse with the established serif wordmark |
| Body and navigation | Manrope: clear, contemporary sans; self-hosted alongside the display font |
| Composition | Asymmetric editorial grid; large headlines, offset image frames, varied section proportions |
| UI treatment | Clear text links and tactile controls; fine rules and generous spacing rather than boxed feature tiles |
| Imagery | New captures and considered crops of the confirmed projects; preserve what the work actually looks like |

Typography is not decorative frosting. Headline line breaks, serif/sans contrast, and relationships between small captions and large statements will establish the composition before animation is added.

Project imagery can bring its own colors. Reachly's page chrome stays coherent around it. The portfolio is not recolored to manufacture a uniform aesthetic.

## The homepage: six acts

### 1. The opening — a bigger stage

**Draft headline:**

> Good businesses deserve a bigger stage.

**Draft supporting copy:**

> We build websites, shape digital presence, and connect the tools behind your business. Thoughtful design. Practical next steps. For businesses in Indonesia.

The first screen is a typographic composition, not a centered slogan over a stock video. The headline spans the canvas with deliberately uneven line lengths. A large circular opening sits off-axis near the lower-right edge, revealing a composed crop of a real project. A small lime point creates a counterweight in the open space. The full frame of the first project is hinted at below the fold.

Navigation is usable immediately. The introduction settles quickly; there is no mandatory preloader or wait to begin reading.

Two clear actions: **Explore the work** and **Discuss your project**.

### 2. The opening becomes the work

The circular window expands as the visitor scrolls and resolves into the first generous project frame. The image remains the same image through the transition, so the movement has continuity rather than feeling like a new animation pasted onto each section.

The rest of the work unfolds as an editorial sequence: a large feature, a contrasting offset feature, and a paired composition. Every project has a visible name, plain-language website subject, and usable link. Initial material:

- **Sellaku** — furniture storefront and product discovery.
- **Mac One Indonesia** — HVACR, engineering, industrial automation, and maintenance services.
- **Crypto Radius** — crypto community, signals, and market context.
- **Crypto Galaxy** — crypto community, programs, research, and membership information.

Capture and assess all four before setting the final sequence; lead with the strongest real visual material. Use actual website captures rather than preserving old device mockups or illustrative previews by default.

Local project pages initially operate as **project notes**, not embellished case studies: real captures, confirmed attribution, website subject, and a direct visit link. Add delivery narratives or outcomes only when supported by further evidence.

### 3. What needs to change? — services as a connected system

A cream chapter introduces five large editorial rows rather than five equal cards:

| Approved service | Visitor-facing explanation |
|---|---|
| Website Design & Development | Make the business clear, credible, and easy to act on |
| Digital Presence | Bring the online touchpoints into alignment |
| Digital Strategy | Decide what matters next, and why |
| Business Automation | Reduce repetitive handoffs between tools |
| SEO | Build a stronger foundation for being found |

Selecting or focusing a row updates an adjacent visual field: a page structure, connected touchpoints, an ordered roadmap, an inquiry-routing diagram, or a search-result/page-structure example. These are transparent explanatory illustrations, not fictitious software screenshots or client results.

A thin reachline connects the five points as the chapter progresses. The point from the opening now represents coordination rather than simply expansion.

A practical prompt—**What needs to change in your business?**—offers a few understandable starting needs, including **I'm not sure yet**. A selection carries context into the project conversation; it does not pretend to diagnose a business or produce a proprietary score.

### 4. How we get there — an unfolding working plan

The process is an annotated working document: **Discover → Plan → Create → Launch → Improve**. It opens in place as the visitor moves through it, with one useful explanation per stage and enough visibility to understand the whole journey.

This is where the site reassures the SME owner: Reachly listens, agrees the scope, makes the solution, checks it, and discusses further improvement where agreed. No invented turnaround time or package promise.

The desktop composition can use a short sticky passage. Mobile gets a deliberate vertical sequence, not a miniature horizontal animation.

### 5. The point of view — designed to be useful

A deep-green editorial pause gives the visitor room to read.

**Draft statement:**

> Beautiful is a beginning. Useful is the point.

Explain Reachly's balance of polished design, clear communication, and appropriate technology. The content is about how Reachly approaches a business—not invented founder history or agency mythology.

This chapter offers a quieter rhythm after the work and service interactions. It should make the agency feel human without fabricating a team, studio location, or manifesto of empty superlatives.

### 6. The invitation — room for your next chapter

The dot expands into a full lime closing canvas.

**Draft headline:**

> What could your business become?

A large **Discuss your project** link leads into the conversation. A compact footer gives clear navigation and verified details when available. The ending completes the opening's visual idea, rather than repeating a generic CTA card.

## Site architecture

- **Home:** the six-act experience.
- **Work:** an art-directed archive of the four confirmed projects, plus a compact text-index view for quick browsing. Four projects do not need a complicated filter system.
- **Project notes:** direct-addressable pages for each confirmed project, with real imagery and honest context.
- **What we do:** the five services in an expanded editorial layout; practical examples and questions a client might ask.
- **About:** Reachly's business focus, approach, and guiding principles, grounded in the company profile.
- **Discuss your project:** a focused, full-page inquiry experience.

Desktop navigation keeps **Work**, **What we do**, and **Discuss your project** visible. The expanded menu offers the complete map in an oversized cream/lime composition. On mobile it becomes a spacious, thumb-friendly menu with the same direct routes.

A work feature can expand into its project page using the same image frame. Browser back, direct links, and refresh remain ordinary working navigation.

## Interaction and motion language

Three signature moments carry most of the expression:

1. **Opening frame:** point → circular window → project frame.
2. **Connected capability:** five service points resolve into a coordinated line.
3. **Closing invitation:** point → full lime canvas.

Smaller movements support these: line-mask headline entrances, gentle image settling, a short underline/arrow response, and image-to-page continuity. The menu opens with a circular sweep related to the same motif. Avoid making every section move in the same direction or reveal on the same timer.

On pointer devices, the aperture can have a slight responsive offset. The native pointer stays familiar; movement is optional, never the way to unlock content. Touch gets an authored sequence rather than cursor emulation.

Reduced-motion mode presents considered, static compositions and immediate state changes. A motion toggle is part of the navigation. Pausing must reveal all content, not freeze it halfway through a mask. This is an alternate designed experience, not a broken cinematic one.

## Technical direction

**Recommendation: Astro, TypeScript, authored CSS, and GSAP/ScrollTrigger for the coordinated scenes.** Generate static routes and keep browser scripts focused on interactions. This site needs art direction and predictable motion, not a large client-side application.

CSS/SVG masks and transforms are the preferred medium for the opening-frame mechanism. Do not begin with a WebGL world merely because the reference pack contains one; the desired visual idea does not depend on it. Native scrolling is the default because it keeps the experience direct and gives scroll-linked animation a dependable input.

Use structured content for services and projects, shared typographic/layout primitives, and scene-specific motion modules with cleanup and responsive reinitialization. Optimize and self-host fonts and imagery. Build semantic content first so routes and information remain useful without animation or JavaScript.

This is a fresh source architecture, not another override layer on the old site's CSS. Once approved, align the repository's older implementation instructions with this commission before coding. Existing business data can inform new content; existing markup, device previews, planner flows, and layout contracts are not preservation obligations.

### The project conversation

Keep the inquiry short: the business, what needs to change, and a reply channel, followed by a review of the brief. Carry any selected service/need into the page. Do not require a visitor to know an exact budget or technical scope to begin.

**One release dependency:** the owner must confirm the real receiving destination and data-handling setup. Design and local verification can proceed before that is supplied. Production must not display a fabricated contact address or claim a brief was delivered when it was not. Final submission behavior and success messaging will be tested against the confirmed receiver.

## Build sequence after approval

1. **Establish the new foundation.** Align project instructions, content structure, static routes, brand assets, and font/imagery provenance. All work stays directly on `reachlyid`; no new implementation branch.
2. **Prove the signature.** Build the opening and first work transition as one complete vertical slice. Review its actual desktop and mobile compositions before repeating the mechanism elsewhere.
3. **Build the editorial journey.** Complete the work sequence, connected services, unfolding process, quiet point-of-view chapter, and closing invitation. Establish section rhythm without relying on effects to hide weak layout.
4. **Complete the website.** Build the archive, project notes, service/about pages, navigation, and inquiry route. Prepare and verify the confirmed inquiry integration when its destination is available.
5. **Art-direct and verify.** Tune type, crop, spacing, transitions, reduced-motion states, and performance in the real browser. Deliver a working local preview with visual evidence. Publication is a separate step.

## What success looks like

- A visitor can identify what Reachly does, who it serves, and the next action from the opening without waiting for an animation. Verify through a first-screen content review and browser exercise.
- All five approved services and all four confirmed projects are discoverable by keyboard, touch, and direct navigation. Verify the actual routes and controls, not just rendered screenshots.
- The opening, work transition, services chapter, and closing each have a distinct composition while reading as one identity. Review captures and live motion on mobile, tablet, desktop, and ultrawide screens.
- Text remains readable and imagery unclipped through the authored transitions, resize, zoom, pause, reduced-motion, and missing-script states. Exercise those states in browser tests and inspect their real appearance.
- The inquiry has clear validation, recovery, and truthful delivery feedback once wired to the confirmed receiver. Verify the receiving effect as well as the local success screen.
- Build, route checks, accessibility checks, and performance measurements pass the agreed implementation gates. Visual quality still requires live review; a test score alone cannot establish that this is an exceptional website.
