## 2. Company Overview

Reachly is a digital presence and technology business focused on helping Indonesian small and medium-sized enterprises build a professional online presence and improve how their digital tools work together.

Its initial service offering combines website design and development, digital presence improvements, strategy, automation, and SEO. The business follows a hybrid direction: deliver tailored services for clients while exploring reusable software products that address recurring business needs.

Reachly aims to pair thoughtful design with clear communication and useful functionality. Its presentation should feel polished and approachable, with a focus on what each business needs to communicate, attract inquiries, and operate more efficiently.

### Mission

Help Indonesian businesses build credible digital experiences and practical systems that support their next stage of growth.

### Vision

Become a trusted digital partner for growing businesses, combining tailored services with useful software products over time.

### Core Value Proposition

Reachly brings design, websites, visibility, strategy, and automation into a coordinated approach, helping business owners make clearer digital decisions and turn those decisions into working solutions.

## 3. Customers and Problems We Serve

The primary audience is Indonesian SMEs and entrepreneurs. Relevant customer groups include:

- **Businesses establishing their first online presence:** Local shops, restaurants, cafes, studios, and service businesses that need a credible digital starting point.
- **Growing businesses improving an existing presence:** SMEs with outdated websites, inconsistent information, or unclear customer inquiry paths.
- **Founders launching a business or brand:** Entrepreneurs and early-stage startups that need a coherent website and digital foundation.
- **Businesses improving everyday workflows:** Teams handling repetitive tasks across inquiries, forms, customer records, and reporting.

Typical problems include unclear service information, fragmented digital channels, limited search visibility, manual administration, and uncertainty about which tools or improvements to prioritize. These are target customer needs, not findings from completed customer research; validated customer research is **TBD**.

## 7. Brand Positioning and Principles

**Positioning statement:** Reachly helps Indonesian businesses build a polished digital presence and practical systems for growth through websites, strategy, visibility, and automation.

Balanced positioning means professional design with a practical, approachable customer experience. Reachly should appeal to SMEs seeking quality and clarity. Actual affordability depends on verified pricing, which remains **TBD**.

Guiding principles:

- **Thoughtful design:** Adapt the experience to each business, its audience, and its goals.
- **Practical value:** Connect design and technology choices to a clear customer or operational need.
- **Clarity:** Explain recommendations, deliverables, costs, and dependencies in understandable language.
- **Appropriate complexity:** Recommend solutions the client can use and sustain.
- **Honest communication:** Keep factual claims accurate and distinguish goals from demonstrated results.

These are operating commitments and positioning principles, not evidence of superior performance or market leadership.
